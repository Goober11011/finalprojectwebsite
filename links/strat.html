Stratagies I will use:

  1. Do A little bit of work everyday

    Until I have no work to do I want to attempt to do at least a little bit of work every day, this can look as short as 30 minutes, just a little bit every day so I don't get out of the flow and academic mindset


  2. Pomodoro Technique

    Along with the previous strategy I also plan on using the Pomodoro technique whenever I need to work or study for extended periods of time. I have never used it before this but I am hoping it can help be avoid getting burnt out after working for too long as well as keeping me from both avoiding homework to relax because I feel I don't have time, and keeping the breaks to a specific length with the timer, both things I've had trouble with in the past. I will likely set each time for work/break longer then the 25/5 depending on the type of work I am attempting to complete


  3. Time Blocking

    Along with with trying the Pomodoro Technique I also want to try to Time Block my future schedules, at least time blocking important times such as work times and study times. If I have each week set up beforehand I'll give me more want to stick to the schedule and avoid procratination. So far using it has also given me joy whenever I've completed something by my blocked time so I am hoping I can continue with that feeling as I use the time blocking.
