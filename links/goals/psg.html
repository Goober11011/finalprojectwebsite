Wake up on time

Wake up at 6-7 everyday, besides the weekends or days off, before the end of the next month for a week

  • SPECIFIC

    I have a specific time of day I want to wake up everyday, aside from my days off, before the end of October for a week (By October 31st)

  • MEASURABLE

    With my specific time to wake up from 6-7 almost every It is pretty easy and clear to measure how I am progressing in this goal, the days I don't count (Weekend, days off) won't count towards my goal. So on the last week of october I am able to wake up from 6-7 on Monday-Thursday, I will have considered myself to have completed this goal

  • ATTAINABLE

    I would say this attainable, I've been waking up fairly consistently at 6-7 the other half of the trouble is to improve how I wake up at that point, going to sleep earlier, better diet, etc. As long as I can keep up the 6-7 I will have completed the goal

  • RELEVANT

    This goal will improve my preformance and timeliness in everything. Because of that I find the goal to be relevant. The wakeup should help but the hope is that with this goal I will force myself to pickup better habits and be overall healthier to accomplish this goal and make it easier because I know if I did a goal of 'Go to sleep early', 'eat healthy' or 'workout consistently' I likely would not be able to get myself to complete these goals. This goal shows me direct results towards want I want to improve (Arrival/better sleep) while hopefully helping me improve in those other aspects

  • TIME-BOUND

    This goal is time-bound because I am forcing myself to complete it before the next month is over, as always as a way to build haste for this goal. The timeframe is especially short because I want to complete it before too far into school so that the early wakeup can directly benefit my school preformance
