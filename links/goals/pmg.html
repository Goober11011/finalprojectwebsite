Keeping up with Mental Health

Over my time while attending school I want to consistently keep up with my mental health in the specific form of medication. I want to keep meeting with my doctor once every 1-3 months. While we meet I want be open on what I want/need and how I feel on whatever it is we are currently trying

  • SPECIFIC

    This goal is specific due to the consistent time of which I want to be meeting, the overall topic of each meeting in medication, and the way I want to discuss the medication; which is trying to be as open and productive as possible, making sure I don't hide how any medication I may be on may or may not be making me feel.

  • MEASURABLE

    This goal is measurable in how often I want to meet with the doctor, the hope is that I will be able to become more settled in what I need by the end of my time at Portland State University

  • ATTAINABLE

    I believe this goal is very attainable, I once every 1-3 months is a lot of time to go between talking, plenty of time to write/think about how I am feeling or any thoughts for the next meeting. Because of the large amount of overall time in by the time I graduate and the time between meeting my doctor this goal should be easy to work towards

  • RELVENT

    This goal is relevent because my mental health will effect my preformance on almost everything, if I can continue to work on it, it will make continuing to improve myself easier and completing work/tasks easier

  • TIME-BOUND

    I want to be able to complete this goal. I have 4 years to do so. I want 4 years so 1. I have enough for whatever it is my timeline is, incase I need more time then expected 2. So that the fact that it is time-bound it forces me to make progress towards this goal
