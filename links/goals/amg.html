Maintain 3.0 GPA

Throughout all my time at PSU I want to maintain a 3.0 GPA

  • SPECIFIC

    This goal is specific due to the clear want of a 3.0 GPA

  • MEASURABLE

    A 3.0 GPA is a clear number goal to reach for. With all the ways to track and calculate your GPA its a pretty easy goal to measure

  • ATTAINABLE

    This goal is attainable, I am shooting for just a 3.0 GPA as opposed to something bigger so that even if things go astray with poor preformance in classes or even fails I pretty easily get back to a 3.0. As well as not creating as much stress

  • RELEVANT

    This is relevant because it is directly linked to my preformance at school then in turn the rest of my life

  • TIME-BOUND

    This goal is time-bound do to the completion of the goal being directly tied to my graduation. If I drop out I immediately fail the goal!
