Attend the Majority of my classes

For the next year I want to attend the majority of my classes, not accounting for sick days, allowing myself five days to miss

  • SPECIFIC

    This goal is specific because I need to make sure I attend each day of scheduled classes for the next year aside from five each month, not accounting for sick days. So It's easy and clear for me to see exactly what I am going for

  • MEASURABLE

    This goal is easily measurable as I have a limit to how much time I am allowing myself to miss, if I go beyond that limit I have not reached my goal

  • ATTAINABLE

    This is attainable. I want to allow myself sick days for free just because I don't know what will come out. I am also allowing myself five days per month because of how much I missed before and because I want to give myself some wiggle room in case I ever have a truely off/break day

  • RELEVENT

    I have failed classes before or had more trouble then I should have due to missed classes, this is one of the largest things I noticed that I want to correct from my past bad habits to make the best out of Portland State University

  • TIME-BOUND

    I am giving myself one year to complete this short goal. This is also part of the reason I decided on five days, I know my habits were really bad before and I want to gradually improve them, hoping to go onto three or below days missed after this year
