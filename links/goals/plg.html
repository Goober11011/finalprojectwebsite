Becoming finacially stable on my own

I want to become finacially stable while only living on my own within a year of graduating, this involves living in my own place, paying for my own utilities, transportation and food. The total cost for the minimum I want should be ~3k a month

  • SPECIFIC

    This goal is specific in the way that I know what I am going for and what I want, not only do I want the money I feel will be necessary but I also want to be able to use that money to achieve everything I feel I need to be self-relient, housing, food, transportation, etc

  • MEASURABLE

    This goal is measurable in two ways, first earning around 3k month, or however much I calculate is necessary to go for this goal over 5 years, and by keeping track of the things I begin to handle myself, food, housing, utilities. I will be able to see my progress as I watch more things fall onto me for example transportation right now

  • ATTAINABLE

    This goal is attainable not only bnecause as I progress my career and degree I should be earning more, but also because I set it as my long goal, giving myself plenty of time to achieve it. With this time I can apply and continue to slowly expand on the things I am paying for myself

  • RELEVANT

    This is realistically my final goal for myself, I feel a want to own much so I feel continuously going for this and making it easier for myself to achieve/transition into it will be useful

  • TIME-BOUND

    I gave myself a year after graduation, I doubt it'll be a whole year but that's just to give myself haste. I want this to be the long term goal because It's not going to be something I want to put my main focus on.
